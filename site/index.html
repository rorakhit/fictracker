Never lose your place in a fic again.

FicTracker is a free browser add-on for AO3. It remembers the chapter you're on in every fic, tells you when your WIPs update, and keeps your reading list in order. No account, no AI, and nothing leaves your browser.

🧩 Add to Chrome 🦊 Add to Firefox

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox and Firefox for Android. Not available for Safari or iPhone/iPad yet.

What it does

🔖

Remembers where you are

Open a chapter on AO3 and FicTracker notes it. "Continue reading" takes you straight back to that chapter, even months later.

📖

Tracks your reading

To read, reading, finished, on hold, dropped, abandoned by the author. Add your own star ratings and notes.

🔔

WIP updates

FicTracker checks your unfinished fics for new chapters a couple of times a day, and the toolbar button shows how many updated. It reads AO3 slowly and politely, one page every few seconds.

📚

Bookshelves

Make shelves by hand ("Comfort reads", "For the long flight"), or save a filter as a smart shelf.

📊

Stats

Your top fandoms and ships, how much you've read, and your reading streaks.

📥

Bring your fics

Import your AO3 history and bookmarks in one go, add fics from the AO3 panel or by URL, or drop in EPUBs you downloaded from AO3.

✨

Follow authors and series

Follow a writer or a series from any fic's page, and FicTracker tells you when something new is posted.

How it works

  • No account. Your library is stored in your browser, inside the add-on. There's no FicTracker server to sign in to.
  • No AI. Nothing in FicTracker is generated or recommended by AI, and your reading is never sent to an AI service.
  • Only talks to AO3. The add-on reads fic details from AO3 pages. It doesn't track you anywhere else and has no analytics.
  • Free. No tiers, no ads, no paid features. Fandom runs on gift culture and so does this.
  • Back it up. Because your data lives in one browser, removing the add-on removes your library. Export a backup file from the Import tab now and then.
