The short version

FicTracker has no account, no server that stores your reading, no analytics and no AI. Your library lives in your browser, inside the FicTracker add-on, and only you can see it.

What FicTracker is

FicTracker is a free browser add-on for Archive of Our Own (AO3) readers. It keeps track of the fics you're reading, the chapter you're on, and which unfinished fics have new chapters. It is built by an independent developer (contact: ro.rakhit@gmail.com).

What the add-on stores, and where

Everything below is kept in your browser's extension storage on your own device. None of it is sent to FicTracker or anyone else.

  • Details of the fics you add: title, authors, fandoms, tags, word and chapter counts, and similar information shown publicly on AO3.
  • Your reading data: statuses, chapter progress, star ratings, notes, shelves, saved filters and a log of chapters read (used for your stats).
  • Your settings, such as reading speed and how the on-page panel is shown.

Removing the add-on deletes all of it. You can also delete everything from the add-on's Settings tab, and export a backup file from its Import tab.

What the add-on connects to

  • archiveofourown.org. The add-on reads fic details from AO3 pages you open, and fetches fic pages from AO3 when you add a fic by URL or check your WIPs for new chapters. Those requests go from your browser to AO3 like any other AO3 page visit, including your AO3 login if you're logged in, so locked works can be read. FicTracker never sees your AO3 password, session or cookies.
  • fictracker.app. When you visit this website with the add-on installed, the add-on copies any library left over from the old FicTracker web app (stored by your browser for this site) into the add-on. Nothing is uploaded; the copy happens inside your browser.

The add-on doesn't run on any other website, doesn't read your browsing history and contains no tracking or analytics.

No AI

FicTracker doesn't use AI for anything, and your reading is never sent to an AI service.

This website

fictracker.app is a static website hosted on Vercel and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, they may log technical details such as your IP address when you load a page. The website itself sets no cookies and stores nothing about you.

The old FicTracker web app and accounts

Before version 2, FicTracker had optional accounts stored with Supabase, a paid tier through Stripe, and an "AI Picks" feature that sent a taste profile (from your highly rated fics) to Anthropic. All of these have been shut down. [CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING: The old account database has been deleted.] If you had an account and have questions about your old data, email ro.rakhit@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy
