FicTracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

The short version

FicTracker has no account, no server that stores your reading, no analytics and no AI. Your library lives in your browser, inside the FicTracker add-on, and only you can see it.

What FicTracker is

FicTracker is a free browser add-on for Archive of Our Own (AO3) readers. It keeps track of the fics you're reading, the chapter you're on, and which unfinished fics have new chapters. It is built by an independent developer (contact: ro.rakhit@gmail.com).

What the add-on stores, and where

Everything below is kept in your browser's extension storage on your own device. None of it is sent to FicTracker or anyone else.

  • Details of the fics you add: title, authors, fandoms, tags, word and chapter counts, and similar information shown publicly on AO3.
  • Your reading data: statuses, chapter progress, star ratings, notes, shelves, saved filters and a log of chapters read (used for your stats).
  • Authors and series you follow, and the works found on their AO3 pages.
  • Your AO3 username, read from AO3's page header when you're logged in, so the add-on can link to your AO3 history and bookmarks. Not your password.
  • Your settings, such as reading speed, how often to check AO3, and how the on-page panel is shown.

Browser sync, if you switch it on. Settings has an option, off by default, to sync your settings, saved Fic Finder filters, smart shelves and the list of authors and series you follow between your own computers. It uses your browser's built-in sync (your Google account in Chrome, Firefox Sync in Firefox), so that data is stored by Google or Mozilla under their privacy policies, the same as your bookmarks when you sync them. FicTracker never sees it. Your library, reading progress, ratings and notes are never synced this way.

Removing the add-on deletes all of it. You can also delete everything from the add-on's Settings tab, and export a backup file from its Import tab.

What the add-on connects to

  • archiveofourown.org. The add-on reads fic details from AO3 pages you open. It also fetches pages from AO3 when you add a fic by URL, import your AO3 history or bookmarks, and when it checks for new chapters and new works from authors and series you follow (automatically, a few times a day while your browser is open, unless you turn that off in Settings). Those requests go from your browser to AO3 like any other AO3 page visit, including your AO3 login if you're logged in, so locked works can be read. FicTracker never sees your AO3 password, session or cookies.
  • fictracker.app. When you visit this website with the add-on installed, the add-on copies any library left over from the old FicTracker web app (stored by your browser for this site) into the add-on. Nothing is uploaded; the copy happens inside your browser.

The add-on doesn't run on any other website, doesn't read your browsing history and contains no tracking or analytics.

No AI

FicTracker doesn't use AI for anything, and your reading is never sent to an AI service.

This website

fictracker.app is a static website hosted on Vercel and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, they may log technical details such as your IP address when you load a page. The website itself sets no cookies and stores nothing about you.

The old FicTracker web app and accounts

Before version 2, FicTracker had optional accounts stored with Supabase, a paid tier through Stripe, and an "AI Picks" feature that sent a taste profile (from your highly rated fics) to Anthropic. All of these have been shut down. The old account database has been deleted, and FicTracker no longer uses Supabase or Stripe at all. If you had an account and have questions about your old data, email ro.rakhit@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the date at the top of this page will change. The aim is always to collect nothing at all.
